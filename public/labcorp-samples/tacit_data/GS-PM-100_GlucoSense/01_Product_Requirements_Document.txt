1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW
─────────────────────────────────────────────
The GlucoSense Patient Monitor is a handheld point-of-care blood glucose meter for
both self-monitoring by patients with diabetes and professional use in clinical settings.
Target: replace older GlucoSense Classic (GS-80) with improved accuracy (ISO 15197:2015
system accuracy), smaller sample volume, Bluetooth connectivity, and multi-language UI.

Target markets: USA, EU, India, Saudi Arabia.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
2. INTENDED USE
─────────────────────────────────────────────
"The GlucoSense Patient Monitor is intended for the quantitative measurement of glucose
in fresh capillary whole blood drawn from the fingertip, palm, or forearm of adults and
children ≥ 2 years of age with diabetes mellitus. The device is intended for self-testing
by patients and for use by healthcare professionals for monitoring of glycaemic control.
The device is not intended for diagnosis of diabetes or for use with venous or arterial blood
without independent validation. FOR IN VITRO DIAGNOSTIC USE ONLY."

─────────────────────────────────────────────
3. PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Parameter                        Target              Acceptance (per ISO 15197:2015)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
System accuracy (< 100 mg/dL)   ≥ 99% within ±15    ≥ 95% within ±15 mg/dL of ref
System accuracy (≥ 100 mg/dL)   ≥ 99% within ±12%   ≥ 95% within ±15% of ref
Measurement range                20–600 mg/dL         20–600 mg/dL
Sample volume                    ≤ 0.5 µL             ≤ 0.6 µL
Time to result                   ≤ 5 seconds          ≤ 10 seconds
Memory                           ≥ 500 results        ≥ 360 results
Battery life                     ≥ 1,000 tests        ≥ 500 tests
Haematocrit range                15–65%               20–60%

─────────────────────────────────────────────
4. DESIGN INPUTS
─────────────────────────────────────────────
4.1 Measurement: Electrochemical amperometric detection, FAD-GDH enzyme on strip electrode.
    (FAD-GDH not affected by oxygen tension — avoids oxygen interference of GOD-based strips)
4.2 Anti-interference: Corrects for haematocrit via dual-channel measurement.
4.3 Display: High-contrast LCD with backlight, readable in bright sunlight.
4.4 UI language: English (default), Arabic, Hindi, German, French, Spanish, Polish selectable.
4.5 Connectivity: USB Micro-B (data download), optional Bluetooth LE 4.2.
4.6 Strip coding: No coding required (factory calibration pre-set on strip chip).
4.7 Data management: 500 test memory with date/time, pre/post meal markers, 14/30-day average.
4.8 Battery: 2 × AAA (user-replaceable). Low battery alert at < 5% remaining.
4.9 Environmental: Operating 10–40°C; storage -20 to +55°C; altitude up to 4,500m.
4.10 Ingress: IP22 (splash-proof from vertical and diagonal directions).
