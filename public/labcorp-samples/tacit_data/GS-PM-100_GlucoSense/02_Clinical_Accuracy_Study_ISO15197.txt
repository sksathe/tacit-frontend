SYSTEM ACCURACY STUDY REPORT (ISO 15197:2015)
====================================
Document No : LC-CAS-GSPM100-002
Product     : GlucoSense Patient Monitor (GS-PM-100) + Test Strips (GS-STRIP-50)
Reference   : YSI 2950 glucose analyzer (NIST-traceable)
Study Sites : LabCorp POC Lab Burlington NC + Atlanta GA Clinical Site
Author      : Dr. Rachel Kim + Dr. Kevin Adeyemi
Date        : 2025-01-20
Status      : FINAL – APPROVED

─────────────────────────────────────────────
1. STUDY DESIGN (ISO 15197:2015 Section 6.3)
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Subjects   : 100 subjects with diabetes (Type 1: 45, Type 2: 53, Gestational: 2)
             + 50 subjects without diabetes (glucose control range)
Age range  : 18–82 years. Pediatric (age 2–17): separate study, n=50.
Specimens  : Fresh capillary blood from fingertip (primary site)
             Forearm and palm results recorded as secondary claims
Glucose range covered: 20–600 mg/dL (enriched at extremes with glucose-spiked samples)
Device lots : 3 production lots tested (Lot A, B, C)
Operators  : 2 trained lab operators + 2 HCP (nurse/physician) + lay users (self-test)

─────────────────────────────────────────────

─────────────────────────────────────────────
5. CONCLUSION
─────────────────────────────────────────────
The GlucoSense Patient Monitor + GS-STRIP-50 test strips meet the system accuracy
requirements of ISO 15197:2015 across the full measurement range, all HCT conditions,
and all interference conditions tested. Results support FDA 510(k) clearance (CLIA Waived
accuracy data) and EU IVDR Class C clinical performance requirements.
