─────────────────────────────────────────────
AGENDA ITEM 1 – Arabic IFU layout for Saudi market
─────────────────────────────────────────────
S. Okonkwo: Arabic IFU requires full RTL (right-to-left) layout. Vendor Al-Mutarjim LLC
confirmed competency. SFDA requires Arabic text approved before market entry.
Design decision: Separate Arabic IFU booklet (not bilingual flip-book) to avoid
layout conflicts. Both EN and AR versions included in KSA packaging.
Action: S. Okonkwo to engage Al-Mutarjim by 2024-08-01.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
AGENDA ITEM 2 – CLIA Waiver status (USA)
─────────────────────────────────────────────
S. Okonkwo: FDA CLIA waiver application requires simplified instructions understandable
by lay users. Usability study with lay users needed (IEC 62366-1 + FDA OTC guidance).
Dr. K. Adeyemi: Will run lay-user study (n=30 untrained subjects) at Burlington site.
Key task: Self-lancing, strip insertion, result reading, meal marker entry.
Design risk: meter UI must be operable without reading IFU (zero-training scenario).
Action: R. Jeon to add animated first-use tutorial to firmware. Due 2024-09-01.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
AGENDA ITEM 3 – Indian Hindi IFU regulatory review
─────────────────────────────────────────────
S. Okonkwo: CDSCO requires paper IFU in English + Hindi (no eIFU exemption).
Back-translation review by Hindi-speaking physician mandatory per CDSCO guidance.
Translation vendor identified: BharatTrans Pvt Ltd, Pune. Regulatory consultant: Dr. Meera Joshi.
Action: S. Okonkwo – engage BharatTrans by 2024-09-15. Dr. Joshi to review final Hindi IFU.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
AGENDA ITEM 4 – Bluetooth LE app compatibility
─────────────────────────────────────────────
R. Jeon: GlucoSense Connect app (iOS 16+ / Android 11+) certified with BT SIG.
Concern raised: some older Android versions (8, 9) still prevalent in India market.
Decision: USB download always supported as mandatory fallback. Bluetooth listed as optional.
IFU to clearly state: "Bluetooth feature requires iOS 16 or Android 11 and above."
Action: R. Jeon – add iOS/Android version requirement to IFU and app store listing.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
AGENDA ITEM 5 – Strip enzyme selection final confirmation
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Dr. R. Kim: FAD-GDH enzyme confirmed. Advantages: no oxygen interference, no
maltose/galactose cross-reactivity (avoids PQQ-GDH issues with peritoneal dialysis patients).
L. Frost: Enzyme sourced from single qualified supplier (Toyobo Japan). Dual-source strategy?
Decision: Initiate qualification of second enzyme supplier (Sekisui Japan) by Q1 2025.
Action: T. Baker to initiate supplier qualification for Sekisui enzyme. Due 2025-03-01.
