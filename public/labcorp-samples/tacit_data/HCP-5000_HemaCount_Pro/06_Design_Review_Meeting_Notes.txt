AGENDA ITEM 2 – Reagent RFID read failure rate
─────────────────────────────────────────────
R. Jeon: Production data shows 0.3% RFID read failure at cold temperatures (< 17°C).
Risk: Operator receives "unrecognised reagent" error at startup in cold labs.
Proposed fix: Software retry loop (3 attempts) before error escalation + warm-up reminder.
L. Frost: Risk acceptable if retry loop documented and failure rate in FMEA updated.
Decision: Implement retry loop in FW 2.1.5. Update FMEA item 3.

ACTION: R. Jeon – implement RFID retry logic. L. Frost – update FMEA.
DUE: 2024-09-15  OWNERS: R. Jeon, L. Frost

─────────────────────────────────────────────
AGENDA ITEM 3 – Regulatory labeling for EU bilingual IFU
─────────────────────────────────────────────
S. Okonkwo: EU IVDR allows IFU on website (eIFU) for professional-use IVDs if
paper summary included. Proposes eIFU for DE, FR, ES, IT to reduce print costs.
Dr. P. Nair: Agreed if paper card with URL and QR code included in box.
Decision: eIFU approach for EU. English, German, French, Spanish, Italian available at
labcorp.com/ifu/hcp5000. Paper card to include URL + QR code + critical safety info.

ACTION: S. Okonkwo – file eIFU notification with TÜV SÜD by 2024-10-01.
DUE: 2024-10-01  OWNER: S. Okonkwo
