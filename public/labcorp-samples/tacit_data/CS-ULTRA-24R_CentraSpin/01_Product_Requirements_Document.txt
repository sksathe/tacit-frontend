─────────────────────────────────────────────
1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW
─────────────────────────────────────────────
The CentraSpin Ultra 24R is a high-performance refrigerated benchtop centrifuge for
clinical and research laboratories. It replaces the legacy CS-3000 model and introduces
significantly improved cooling performance, a wider rotor ecosystem, and digital
connectivity for laboratory information integration. Complies with IEC 61010-2-020
(laboratory centrifuges) safety requirements globally.

Target markets: USA, European Union, United Kingdom, China (NMPA).

─────────────────────────────────────────────
2. INTENDED USE
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Intended for separation and processing of biological specimens including whole blood,
serum, plasma, urine sediment, cell cultures, and density gradient separations in
clinical and research laboratory environments. Operated by trained laboratory personnel.
NOT intended for ultracentrifugation. NOT for use with flammable or explosive materials.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
4. DESIGN INPUTS
─────────────────────────────────────────────
4.1 Motor: Brushless DC maintenance-free motor rated to 20,000 RPM with imbalance detection.
4.2 Lid interlock: Electronic AND mechanical dual-lock; prevents lid opening while rotor moving.
4.3 Rotor identification: RFID chip on each rotor; software enforces speed limits per rotor type.
4.4 Refrigeration: Eco-friendly HFO-1234yf refrigerant (GWP < 1). No CFCs.
4.5 Bowl: Stainless steel with decontamination drain port.
4.6 Display: Backlit LCD, rotor profile storage (up to 50 user-defined programs).
4.7 Connectivity: RS-232 serial port for data logging to LIMS.
4.8 Safety: Automatic rotor over-speed protection; unbalance detection shuts down at 20% imbalance.
4.9 Certifications required: UL (USA/CA), CE Machinery Directive (EU), UKCA (UK), NMPA (CN).
4.10 Foot print: Benchtop ≤ 450mm × 600mm. Weight ≤ 50kg.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
5. REGULATORY CLASSIFICATION
─────────────────────────────────────────────
USA     : Not regulated as medical device (general lab equipment). Requires UL 61010-2-020 listing.
EU      : CE marking under Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC (self-declaration). EN 61010-2-020.
UK      : UKCA self-declaration (post-Brexit). BS EN 61010-2-020:2019.
China   : NMPA Class II medical device registration (NMPA regulates centrifuges used clinically).
Canada  : CSA C22.2 No. 61010-2-020. Included in UL joint certification.
