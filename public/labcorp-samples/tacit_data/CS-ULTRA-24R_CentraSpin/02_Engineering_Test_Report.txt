─────────────────────────────────────────────
3. NOISE LEVEL
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Measured at 1m distance, operator position, per IEC 61010-2-020 method.
Full rotor (24 × 50mL, water-filled, balanced), 15,000 RPM.
Measured: 51.3 dB(A). Specification: < 55 dB(A). PASS.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
4. IMBALANCE DETECTION AND SHUTDOWN
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Protocol: Introduce 10g, 20g, 30g, 50g imbalance loads. Verify shutdown trigger.
10g imbalance: vibration flag after 30 sec. Alert displayed. No shutdown.
20g imbalance: vibration flag after 15 sec. Warning alert. No shutdown.
30g imbalance: shutdown triggered at 8,000 RPM within 45 sec. PASS (> 20% threshold).
50g imbalance: shutdown triggered at 3,200 RPM within 12 sec. PASS.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
5. LID INTERLOCK SAFETY TEST
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Test: Attempt to open lid at multiple speed points during a spin.
Result: Lid locked at all speeds > 0 RPM. Electronic interlock + mechanical bolt engaged.
Manual override (emergency key): Functions correctly; only accessible by trained tech.
PASS per IEC 61010-2-020 clause 8.4.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
6. ROTOR RFID ENFORCEMENT
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Tested: Rotor A (max 15,000 RPM) and Rotor B (max 6,000 RPM, swing-bucket).
Scenario: Set run to 10,000 RPM with Rotor B installed.
Result: System read RFID chip, identified max 6,000 RPM for Rotor B,
        auto-capped set speed to 6,000 RPM, displayed warning to operator.
PASS — speed limit enforcement verified for all 8 rotor types in product family.
