2. MASTER MIX FORMULATION (PROPRIETARY – CONFIDENTIAL)
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Base            : One-Step RT-PCR master mix (LabCorp in-house)
RT enzyme       : MMLV reverse transcriptase variant (thermostable, 55°C optimum)
DNA polymerase  : Hot-start Taq with anti-Taq antibody (specificity enhancement)
dNTPs           : 0.2 mM each
MgCl2           : 3.5 mM (optimized for multiplex)
Formamide       : 1.5% v/v (reduces secondary structure in GC-rich targets)
Enhancers       : BSA 0.1 mg/mL, Betaine 0.5M, DMSO 3%

Note: Formamide requires GHS reproductive toxicant labeling (H361) on all packaging.
Refer to SDS: LC-SDS-PDRTPCR-MM-001. Operators must be informed via IFU.
