CLINICAL PERFORMANCE STUDY REPORT
====================================
Document No : LC-CPS-PDRTPCR-003
Product     : PathDetect RT-PCR Kit (PD-RTPCR-96)
Study Type  : Prospective multi-site clinical performance evaluation
Sites       : USA (n=2), EU Germany (n=1), EU Netherlands (n=1)
Author      : Dr. Kevin Adeyemi, Clinical Affairs
Date        : 2024-12-15
Status      : FINAL – APPROVED

─────────────────────────────────────────────
1. STUDY DESIGN
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Objective: Establish clinical sensitivity and specificity vs. comparator method (CDC
           approved multiplex RT-PCR or individual gold-standard assays per target).
Specimens: Archived residual NPS/OPS specimens from symptomatic patients
           (influenza season Oct 2023 – Mar 2024)
Total N   : 1,847 specimens
Positives : Flu A (n=312), Flu B (n=187), RSV (n=203), SARS-CoV-2 (n=289)
Negatives : 856 specimens negative for all four targets

─────────────────────────────────────────────
2. CLINICAL PERFORMANCE RESULTS
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Target          Sensitivity (95% CI)      Specificity (95% CI)      PPV      NPV
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Influenza A     98.7% (96.6–99.6%)        99.5% (98.9–99.8%)        98.4%    99.6%
Influenza B     98.4% (95.5–99.6%)        99.7% (99.2–99.9%)        98.9%    99.5%
RSV A/B         97.5% (94.3–99.2%)        99.3% (98.7–99.7%)        97.1%    99.5%
SARS-CoV-2      99.3% (97.6–99.9%)        99.8% (99.4–100%)         99.7%    99.5%

All targets meet pre-defined acceptance criteria (sensitivity ≥ 95%, specificity ≥ 97%).

─────────────────────────────────────────────
3. DISCORDANT RESULT ANALYSIS
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Influenza A discordants (n=4 false negatives, n=8 false positives comparator):
  - 4 FN: all had Ct > 38 in comparator (very low viral load), suggest near-LoD samples.
    Resolution: Specimens re-extracted; PathDetect confirmed negative → clinically acceptable.
  - 8 FP comparator: PathDetect positive, comparator negative. Re-tested with third method
    (sequencing). PathDetect correct in 7/8 cases → improved sensitivity over comparator.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
4. REPRODUCIBILITY (MULTI-SITE)
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Panel of 20 specimens (10 positive at 3× LoD, 10 negative) tested at all 4 sites × 3 days
× 2 operators × 2 instruments per site.

Overall agreement: 100% across all sites, operators, days, instruments.
Between-site Ct CV: FluA 1.2%, FluB 1.4%, RSV 1.3%, CoV2 0.9% — all ≤ 2% specification.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
5. INTERNAL CONTROL PERFORMANCE
─────────────────────────────────────────────
MS2 internal control detected in 100% of valid runs.
IC inhibition observed in 3/1847 specimens (0.16%) – all correctly flagged as invalid.
Re-extraction resolved inhibition in all 3 cases.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
6. CONCLUSION
─────────────────────────────────────────────
The PathDetect RT-PCR Kit demonstrates clinical sensitivity and specificity meeting
or exceeding regulatory acceptance criteria for all four targets across all four sites.
The assay is suitable for professional laboratory use for detection of respiratory
pathogens in symptomatic patients. Results support FDA De Novo application and IVDR
Class C technical file.
