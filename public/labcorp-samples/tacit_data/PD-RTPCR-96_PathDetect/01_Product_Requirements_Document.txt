1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW
─────────────────────────────────────────────
The PathDetect RT-PCR Kit is a multiplexed qualitative real-time RT-PCR assay for the
simultaneous detection and differentiation of four respiratory pathogens from a single
patient specimen. Developed in response to post-pandemic demand for consolidated
respiratory panel testing and shortage of supply-chain-robust domestic assay options.

Target pathogens: Influenza A, Influenza B, RSV A/B, SARS-CoV-2
Target markets  : USA (De Novo), EU (IVDR Class C), Japan (Shonin), Brazil (ANVISA)

─────────────────────────────────────────────
2. INTENDED USE (DRAFT)
─────────────────────────────────────────────
"The PathDetect RT-PCR Kit is a qualitative in vitro nucleic acid amplification test
for the detection and differentiation of Influenza A RNA, Influenza B RNA, Respiratory
Syncytial Virus (RSV) A and B RNA, and SARS-CoV-2 RNA in nasopharyngeal swab (NPS)
and oropharyngeal swab (OPS) specimens collected from symptomatic patients presenting
with signs and symptoms of respiratory infection. The assay is intended for use on
compatible real-time PCR instruments in professional laboratory settings.
A negative result does not exclude infection. FOR PROFESSIONAL USE ONLY."

3. PERFORMANCE TARGETS
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Metric                       Target         Minimum Acceptance
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Clinical sensitivity         ≥ 98%          ≥ 95%
Clinical specificity         ≥ 99%          ≥ 97%
LoD (each target)            ≤ 250 copies/mL As low as feasible
Time from extraction to result ≤ 90 min     ≤ 120 min
Lot-to-lot CV (Ct value)     ≤ 2%           ≤ 3%
Stability (unopened, -20°C)  18 months      12 months

─────────────────────────────────────────────
4. DESIGN INPUTS
─────────────────────────────────────────────
4.1 Primer/probe design: conserved regions, TaqMan chemistry, FAM/HEX/ROX/Cy5 channels
4.2 Internal control: MS2 phage RNA – monitors extraction efficiency and inhibition
4.3 Controls included per kit: positive control (synthetic RNA), negative control (water)
4.4 Compatible instruments: Applied Biosystems 7500 Fast, Bio-Rad CFX96, Roche LightCycler 480
4.5 Extraction compatibility: validated with QIAGEN EZ1, Thermo MagMAX, Roche MagnaPure
4.6 Kit format: 96-well plate (8×12), foil-sealed reaction strips
4.7 Cold chain: -20°C transport and storage; components stable 8 hrs at 4°C during setup
4.8 Formamide in master mix: must carry reproductive toxicant labeling (GHS08 pictogram)
